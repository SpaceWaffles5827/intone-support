Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Intone does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no Intone account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours behind the app.

Your training history

Your training history (which levels you have played, your answers, streaks and statistics) and your settings are saved on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to us: we have no server to send them to, and we cannot see them.

iCloud

So that your history survives deleting the app and follows you to a new iPhone, Intone keeps a second copy of it in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That copy is private to your Apple Account and is handled by Apple under Apple's terms; it is not available to us or to anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Your data in the app; your history then stays only on your iPhone. Your settings are not stored in iCloud.

Backup files

You can export your history as a file from Settings › Your data, and import it again later. An exported file goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you currently have Intone Pro; that answer stays on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on practice reminders, they are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No push notification service is used and no device identifier is sent anywhere.

Sound

Every sound is produced on your device. Intone does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Third parties

Intone contains no third-party software development kits, trackers or advertising networks. It contacts no server of ours or of any third party; the only services it uses are Apple's, for purchases and for the iCloud copy described above.

Children

Intone does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Your data › Reset Progress in the app to erase your training history from your iPhone and from the copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what it stored on your iPhone but not the iCloud copy, which is what lets your history come back if you reinstall; reset your progress first if you want that gone too. Backup files you exported are yours to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
