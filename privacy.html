Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Intone does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server behind the app.

What stays on your device

Your training history (which levels you have played, your answers, streaks and statistics) and your settings are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup under Apple's terms, so it can be restored to a new device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you currently have Intone Pro; that answer stays on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on practice reminders, they are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No push notification service is used and no device identifier is sent anywhere.

Sound

Every sound is generated on your device. Intone does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Third parties

Intone contains no third-party software development kits, trackers or advertising networks, and makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Intone does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset progress in the app to erase your training history, or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
