Common questions

I can't hear anything

Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Intone plays even when the silent switch is on. If you use Bluetooth headphones, check that they are connected. Very low notes are quiet on a phone speaker, so headphones are recommended.

I bought Intone Pro but levels are still locked

Open Settings › Intone Pro › Restore purchases while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › Intone Pro › Manage subscription in the app, or the iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free for new subscribers. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged and the subscription renews each year until cancelled.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. Progress is stored only on your device; there is no account to sign in to. Purchases are restored through the App Store.

Will Intone give me perfect pitch?

Intone trains you to recognise notes, intervals and chords by ear, and regular practice makes most people noticeably faster and more accurate. It does not promise anyone the effortless absolute pitch that some people develop in early childhood.
